### What changes under 8VAC20-781
Per the proposed text and trade analysis ([Register](https://register.dls.virginia.gov/details.aspx?id=12032); [Famly](https://www.famly.co/us/blog/whats-changing-in-virginia-licensing-standards-in-2026)):

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| **Ratios** | Core ratios **unchanged**. School-age loosened: **max 20 school-age children per staff member (up from 18)**; **max group size 100 school-age** |
| **Space** | 35 sq ft/child indoor; 75 sq ft/child outdoor (unchanged) |
| **Staff qualifications** | **Loosened** — director and lead teacher pathways expanded to include **career studies certificates** and **Virginia endorsements in child-related fields** |
| **Training** | **3 of the 16** required annual hours must be VDOE's health & safety update course; new-staff orientation expands from **11 to 20 topics**; directors must complete department pre-licensure orientation **within 60 days** of hire/promotion; the 10-hour preservice orientation now counts toward first-year ongoing training |
| **Screen time (NEW)** | Visual media **prohibited** birth–16 months; **max 2 hrs/day** for 16–24 months and 2+ (unless curriculum-based) |
| **Safe sleep** | Swaddling, weighted blankets, sleep-adaptive equipment **prohibited** absent physician order |
| **Supervision** | Sight-and-sound at all times for under-10; restroom exception with 5-minute check |
| **Building** | Lead risk assessments for pre-1978 buildings; CO detectors for pre-2015 buildings; potable water testing plan or FDA-compliant bottled water |
| **Emergency** | Lockdown drills **2x/year** (up from 1x); emergency care plans for any condition likely to cause a medical emergency |
| **Infant care** | Diaper checks **at least every 2 hours** |
| **Swim** | 1 lifeguard per 25 children in water |
| **Records** | Parents must **annually attest** to accuracy of child records, documented |

**Operational read:** the qualification loosening is a genuine hiring tailwind. Everything else is added cost and documentation load — screen-time limits, doubled lockdown drills, annual parent attestations, and building assessments are the items most likely to generate citations in the first inspection cycle.

### Rate methodology — cost-based, not market-rate
Virginia is one of the leading states using an **alternative methodology (cost estimation model)** rather than a market rate survey, developed with **Prenatal to Five Fiscal Strategies** and directed by the 2022 Special Session I budget to satisfy CCDBG Act of 2014 requirements. Rates reflect **program inputs** — wages, program standards, curriculum, quality improvement — and vary by **child age, setting type, and regional grouping** rather than individual locality market. ([Child Care VA cost model](https://www.childcare.virginia.gov/reports-resources/research-reports-and-resource/virginia-s-cost-estimation-model); [Voices](https://vakids.org/posts/voices-supports-proposed-changes-to-child-care-payment-rates-and-parent-co-pays))

**🔴 THE CENTRAL PROVIDER GRIEVANCE: rates have not increased since October 2022.** Cost-model regional rates took effect October 2022 (Phase I); the published "Maximum Reimbursement Rates by Locality" schedule is dated **effective August 2023**. Multiple 2026 sources confirm reimbursement rates **remain unchanged since 2022**. ([VPM, June 26, 2026](https://www.vpm.org/generalassembly/2026-06-26/child-care-subsidy-program-virginia-education-gilbreath-bowles-goren); [VPM, Feb. 24, 2026](https://www.vpm.org/generalassembly/2026-02-24/child-care-funding-formula-ccsp-locke-conway-convirs-fowler-goren-sewell); [Child Care VA vendor page](https://www.childcare.virginia.gov/providers/child-care-subsidy/current-child-care-subsidy-vendors)) Four years of wage and insurance inflation have been absorbed by providers.

## 4. QUALITY RATING SYSTEM — VQB5

**VQB5 (Virginia Quality Birth to Five) is mandatory for every site receiving public early childhood funds** — and is unusual nationally in that it measures *all* publicly funded birth-to-five programs on a single instrument.

**Mandatory participants** ([VDOE VQB5 Participation Requirements](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/teaching-learning-assessment/early-childhood-care-education/vqb5-participation-requirements)): VPI, ECSE, Title I Preschool, Head Start/Early Head Start, **Child Care Subsidy Program**, CCAMPIS, and Military Child Care Fee Assistance (MCCYN). Non-publicly-funded programs may participate voluntarily to obtain a public quality profile.

**→ Operationally: if the client accepts a single subsidy child at a site, that site is in VQB5 and gets a publicly posted rating.** This is the key strategic consideration — subsidy participation carries a public-reputation obligation that vouchers in most states do not.

### Annual requirements — three obligations per site

**1. LinkB5 registration and profiles.** Registration window **August 15 – October 1**; site, administrator, educator, and classroom profiles. Curriculum reported by **October 1**, updatable through **May 31**.

**2. Local CLASS observations.** **Every eligible classroom** completes **two** local observations annually — **fall (Aug 15 – Dec 22)** and **spring (Jan 20 – May 31)** — with scores entered into LinkB5. These are conducted by the program's own certified observers.

**3. External CLASS observations.** Each site participates in external observations for **each age level served**, Aug 15 – May 31, covering approximately **75% of classrooms**.

**CLASS tools by age:** Infant CLASS (birth–18 mo); Toddler CLASS (15–36 mo); Pre-K CLASS (3–5 yrs). **Mixed-age settings use the Toddler tool.**

### Rating structure — methodology changing for fall 2027
Per the [VQB5 Participation Handbook](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/54642/638857488545630000), a points-based system totaling **800 points** produces five levels:

| Level | Points |
|---|---|
| **Distinguished** | 700–800 |
| **Exceeds Expectations** | 600–699 |
| **Meets Expectations** | 500–599 |
| **Approaching Expectations** | 400–499 |
| **Needs Support** | 100–399 |

- **Interactions: 700 points** = average of all local CLASS scores (fall + spring, all VQB5-eligible classrooms at the site) **× 100**
- **Curriculum: 100 points** = full points only if an **approved curriculum is used in every classroom** by May 31

**Consequence:** sites scoring **499 or below** face **mandatory structured improvement planning beginning fall 2027**.

**Public reporting:** Quality Profiles publish **each fall** at [EarlyChildhoodQuality.doe.virginia.gov](https://earlychildhoodquality.doe.virginia.gov/). Downloadable datasets are public — meaning **competitors, journalists, and parents can pull the client's site-level scores.**

⚠️ The handbook text retrieved describes the five-level points methodology as taking effect **fall 2027**. Whether the currently published (fall 2025/fall 2026) profiles use the same five labels under a different calculation is **UNCONFIRMED** — the VQB5 FAQ and Quality Profiles pages are robots-blocked/404. Verify the current-year methodology before benchmarking.

### What this means operationally for a for-profit operator
1. **Real labor cost.** Two local CLASS observations per classroom per year requires certified in-house observers. CLASS certification and annual recertification is a budgeted, recurring staff-development expense at every site.
2. **The score is the rating.** 700 of 800 points is pure CLASS interaction quality. There is no compliance-paperwork path to a good rating — it is teacher-child interaction, which means it is coaching, retention, and ratio stability.
3. **Curriculum is 100 free points.** Adopting a VDOE-approved curriculum **in every classroom** and reporting it by October 1 is the cheapest 12.5% of the score available. Any site not doing this is leaving points on the table. Confirm approved-list status of the client's national curriculum.
4. **Turnover is the enemy twice over** — it depresses CLASS scores and forces re-certification of observers.
5. **Reputational exposure is public and permanent.** A "Needs Support" profile is discoverable by every prospective family.
6. **Mixed-age rooms are scored on the Toddler tool** — a scheduling/room-configuration decision with direct rating consequences.
7. **RecognizeB5** is a companion VDOE educator incentive program (**$20M, FY25**) — a retention lever the client should be enrolling staff in.

**Virginia's own claim: 99% of early childhood sites meet quality expectations** ([VDOE](https://www.doe.virginia.gov/about-vdoe/agency-leadership/superintendent)). Read that as: a good rating is table stakes, not a differentiator; a bad one is a serious liability.
